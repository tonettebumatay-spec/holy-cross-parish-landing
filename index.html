About Holy Cross Parish

Holy Cross Parish Alcala, Pangasinan

Established in 1881, Holy Cross Parish Church in Alcala, Pangasinan, is a historic Roman Catholic church under the Diocese of Urdaneta. Dedicated to the Holy Cross, it serves as a place of worship, prayer, and spiritual guidance for the local community.

Known for its religious heritage and devotion to the Holy Cross, the parish remains a significant spiritual landmark and pilgrimage destination for Catholics in Pangasinan and nearby communities.

Explore Holy Cross Parish

THE CONVENT

The historic convent of Holy Cross Parish in Alcala, Pangasinan, traces its beginnings to the parish's establishment in 1881. Its construction was initiated by Fr. Eduardo Samaniego, O.P., and continued by fellow Dominican priests. It stands as part of the parish's rich religious heritage.

THE ALTAR

The main altar of Holy Cross Parish serves as the heart of the church's worship and prayer. Dedicated to the Holy Cross, it reflects the parish's deep devotion to Christ and stands as a sacred focal point for the celebration of the Holy Eucharist.

THE RELIQUIARY

Holy Cross Parish houses a sacred relic of the Holy Cross, preserved for the veneration of the faithful. Located in a side chapel, the reliquary serves as a meaningful destination for prayer and devotion, reflecting the parish's deep faith and religious heritage.
